## 1/ Cinematic upgrade
Use this when you want a normal video to look more professional.
Prompt:
Transform this video into a high-quality cinematic sequence. Keep [main subject] completely unchanged, including identity, appearance, movement, and key details. Enhance the scene with [lighting style], [camera movement], [colour grading], and [visual mood]. Improve depth, contrast, realism, and overall production quality. Do not change [details that must stay the same].
Example:
Transform this video into a high-quality cinematic sequence. Keep my face, outfit, and body movement completely unchanged. Enhance the scene with warm sunset lighting, slow cinematic camera movement, soft contrast, rich colour grading, and a luxury calm mood. Improve depth and realism. Do not change the background or my natural movements.

## 2/ Background replacement
Use this when you want to change the location.
Prompt:
Replace the existing background with [new location]. Keep [main subject] exactly the same, including face, body, clothing, movement, and identity. Match the new environment with realistic lighting, shadows, reflections, depth, and camera perspective. Make the transition look natural and seamless. Do not change [important details].
Example:
Replace the background with a modern futuristic AI studio. Keep the speaker exactly the same, including face, clothing, voice, and lip movement. Match the studio lighting, shadows, reflections, and camera angle so it looks completely realistic. Do not change the person or their movements.

## 5/ Outfit change
Use this when you want to change clothing in a video.
Prompt:
Change [person's outfit] into [new outfit style]. Preserve the person's face, identity, body shape, hairstyle, pose, and movement exactly. Make the new clothing fit naturally with realistic fabric texture, folds, shadows, and motion. Match the original lighting and environment.
Example:
Change my outfit into a premium black luxury streetwear style. Preserve my face, identity, body shape, pose, and movement exactly. Make the clothing move naturally with realistic fabric details, shadows, and folds. Match the lighting and environment of the original video.

## 4/ Object replacement
Use this when you want to swap one object for another.
Prompt:
Replace [old object] with [new object] while preserving the original scene composition. Keep the camera angle, lighting, perspective, and movement unchanged. Make [new object] blend naturally into the environment with realistic textures, shadows, reflections, and motion. Do not change [main subject] or [background].
Example:
Replace the plastic cup with a futuristic transparent glass cube. Keep the same camera angle, lighting, and scene composition. Make the cube match the environment with realistic reflections, shadows, and movement. Do not change the person or background.

## 3/ Object removal
Use this when you want to remove something from the video.
Prompt:
Remove [object to remove] from this video completely. Reconstruct the missing area using realistic background details from the surrounding scene. Keep [main subject], lighting, shadows, camera movement, and overall composition unchanged. Ensure the removal looks natural with no visible editing artifacts.
Example:
Remove the water bottle from this video completely. Reconstruct the area using the surrounding table texture and background details. Keep the person, lighting, shadows, and camera movement unchanged. Ensure the final result looks clean and realistic.

## Don't settle for a generic AI-generated video. Guide the visuals, pacing, atmosphere, and key ideas with these prompts.
1. Educational or Documentary Videos
"Create a cinematic educational video based on this document. Use a [dark, moody, bright, or minimalist] visual style. Prioritize [Key Topic 1] and [Key Topic 2]. Use dynamic motion, clear visual explanations, and code-inspired animations where appropriate to illustrate complex statistics or data. Keep the narration coherent and the transitions smooth and professional."
2. Storytelling or Mystery Videos
"Transform this document into an engaging, fast-paced cinematic video. Use a [cyberpunk, historical, futuristic, or cinematic realism] aesthetic. Highlight the mystery and intrigue surrounding [Specific Event or Concept]. Use dramatic framing, strategic zoom-ins, and smooth camera movements to build suspense while keeping the story easy to follow."
3. Step-by-Step Tutorial Videos
"Create a clear instructional video based on this document. Break the process into simple, logical steps that viewers can easily follow. Use a [clean, modern, or corporate] visual style. Illustrate [Specific Action or Object] with clear, relevant visuals. Emphasize the key takeaways and guide viewers toward [Desired Outcome]."
