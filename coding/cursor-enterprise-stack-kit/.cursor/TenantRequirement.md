7 CORE PROGRAMMES
==================================================

1. ALL-IN CHEMISTRY SPM 2026

Complete Chemistry preparation programme for SPM 2026.


2. DLP CHEMISTRY PROGRAM 2027

Long-term DLP Chemistry programme from November 2026 until December 2027.


3. PECUTAN SPM — OCTOBER 2026

Special SPM programme on 24 & 25 October 2026.


4. KELAS BERKUMPULAN 2027

Online group classes for Tingkatan 4 and Tingkatan 5 focusing on Science Stream and Core Subjects.


5. RECORDING PURCHASE

STATUS:
Always available according to current recording availability.

Students may purchase recordings currently uploaded/listed and approved for sale by Confirm Pandai Academy.

The recording list and prices may change over time.


6. SUBJECT EVENTS

STATUS:
Ongoing programme category.

Subject Events may be announced at any time by Confirm Pandai Academy.

They are online group classes conducted through Google Meet.

Events may be held:

• During weekends
• During school holidays
• At selected times
• During examination preparation
• During special campaigns

An event may include one or multiple subjects.

Registration opens according to each announced event.

There is no permanent Subject Event price.


7. PERSONAL 1-TO-1 CLASSES

STATUS:
Open for request at any time.

Parents or students may request personal tuition at any time.

FORMAT:
1 Student + 1 Tutor

MODE:
Online

Available for:
• Primary
• Secondary
• Other supported levels

Available for multiple subjects according to tutor expertise and availability.

The actual tutor, schedule and fee must be confirmed for each request.
