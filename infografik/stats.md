Prompt: "Convert [specific subject you want to explain] into a 6 slide carousel. Create a curiosity driven opening, valuable copy for each slide, and a memorable final takeaway. Give every slide a distinct visual treatment while keeping the story connected, polished, easy to scan, and impossible to abandon."

## 3. Informative Infographic Layout
Prompt: "Build [facts, statistics, or information] into a professional infographic. Identify the strongest insights, group related information, and create a clear visual hierarchy. Recommend charts, icons, labels, and spacing that make complicated information feel simple, attractive, balanced, and instantly understandable."

## 4. Shareable Quote Concept
Prompt: "Transform [thought, statement, or personal insight] into a powerful quote graphic. Refine the wording into a memorable one liner, then build a fitting visual style around it. Choose typography, background treatment, alignment, spacing, and emphasis that make the message emotional, premium, distinctive, and highly shareable."

## 5. Premium Product Feature

## 7. Simple Educational Visual
Prompt: "Explain [specific concept or lesson] through one highly engaging educational graphic. Extract three genuinely useful points, create a curiosity driven headline, and pair each point with a simple visual cue. Keep the information concise while making the design memorable, visually balanced, and effortless to understand."

## 8. Before After Presentation
Prompt: "Present [specific transformation or comparison] as a compelling before and after graphic. Create a strong headline, concise labels, and one supporting message that reinforces the change. Use visual contrast, balanced composition, clear separation, and strategic emphasis to make the transformation immediately obvious and satisfying."

## 10. Complete Campaign Strategy
Prompt: "Plan a 5 post graphic series around [specific campaign theme or content goal].
