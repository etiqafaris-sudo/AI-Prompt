## 1) Brand Identity Logo
Act as a senior brand identity designer.
Create 5 distinct logo concepts for [BRAND NAME], a [BUSINESS TYPE].
Brand personality: [3–5 TRAITS]. Target audience: [AUDIENCE]. Desired style: [STYLE].
For each concept, define: — Core visual idea
— Symbol/concept
— Typography direction
— Color palette
— Why it fits the brand
Keep every concept simple, recognizable, scalable, and usable across digital and print.

## 2) Minimalist Logo
Design a minimalist logo for [BRAND NAME].
Reduce the brand concept to one memorable visual idea.
Use simple geometry, strong negative space, restrained typography, and no unnecessary details.
The logo must work at favicon size and remain recognizable in black and white.
Avoid generic AI imagery, excessive gradients, 3D effects, shadows, and decorative elements.
Provide 5 different directions with a short rationale for each.

## 3) Luxury Logo
Create a premium luxury logo identity for [BRAND NAME], a [BUSINESS].
Position the brand as [PREMIUM / EXCLUSIVE / REFINED].
Explore sophisticated typography, balanced proportions, subtle symbolism, and restrained color usage.
The design should feel timeless rather than trendy.
Create 5 concepts and explain the design logic behind each.
Avoid crowns, generic monograms, excessive gold effects, and cliché luxury symbols.

## 4) Wordmark Logo
Design a professional wordmark logo for [BRAND NAME].
Focus entirely on typography and distinctive letterform treatment.
Explore:
Geometric sans-serif
Humanist sans-serif
Editorial serif
Custom letterforms
Subtle typographic symbol integration
Keep the result highly legible, distinctive, scalable, and suitable for a modern brand identity.
Explain which letters or proportions can become recognizable brand assets.

## 5) Symbol + Wordmark
Develop a complete logo system for [BRAND NAME].
Create: — Standalone symbol — Wordmark — Symbol + wordmark lockup — Horizontal version — Stacked version — Monochrome version
The symbol should communicate [CORE IDEA] without literally illustrating it.
Prioritize simplicity, recognition, balance, and scalability.
Define the visual reasoning behind the system.
